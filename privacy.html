Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains what information PlumBot may process and how it is used.

Last updated • September 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

PlumBot may process and store limited information necessary to provide its Discord features.

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server (guild) IDs
  • Discord channel and role IDs
  • Moderation information, including warnings
  • Server-specific configuration and settings
  • Information necessary to process commands and interactions

PlumBot does not intentionally collect Discord passwords, payment information, or other account credentials.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by PlumBot may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain Bot features
  • Process commands and interactions
  • Provide moderation functionality
  • Store server-specific settings
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the Bot

3. Data Sharing

PlumBot does not sell users' personal information.

Certain information may be displayed to authorized members of a Discord server when required by a feature, such as moderation records.

Information may also be processed by services required to operate PlumBot, including Discord and the Bot's hosting provider.

4. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide PlumBot's features.

Moderation records and server configuration may remain stored until they are deleted, reset, or no longer required.

5. Data Removal

Users and Discord server administrators may request deletion of data associated with them or their server by contacting the PlumBot administrator.

Contact us on Discord

6. Discord

PlumBot operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PlumBot's features or data practices change. The date displayed at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

8. Contact

For questions, privacy concerns, or data deletion requests, contact the PlumBot administrator through the support server.
