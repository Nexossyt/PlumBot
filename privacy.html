PlumBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PlumBot ("the Bot") processes and stores information when you use the Bot on Discord.

1. Information We Collect

PlumBot may process or store limited information necessary for its features, including:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server (guild) IDs
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Discord role IDs
  • Moderation information, such as warnings and related moderation records
  • Server-specific configuration and settings
  • Information required to process commands and interactions

PlumBot does not intentionally collect Discord passwords, payment information, or other account credentials.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by PlumBot may be used to:

  • Provide the Bot's features
  • Process commands and interactions
  • Provide moderation features
  • Store server-specific settings
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the Bot

3. Data Sharing

PlumBot does not sell users' personal information.

Certain information may be displayed to authorized members of a Discord server when required by a feature, such as moderation records.

Information may also be processed by services necessary to operate PlumBot, including Discord and the Bot's hosting provider.

4. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide PlumBot's features.

Some information, such as moderation records or server configuration, may remain stored until it is deleted, reset, or no longer required.

5. Data Removal

Users and Discord server administrators may request the deletion of data associated with them or their server by contacting the PlumBot administrator.

Requests can be made through the PlumBot support server:

https://discord.gg/5nwRMJxxSk

6. Discord

PlumBot operates through Discord. Use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PlumBot's features or data practices change. The date displayed at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

8. Contact

For questions, privacy concerns, or data deletion requests, contact the PlumBot administrator through the support server:
